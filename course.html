CSE 203 Digital Logic Design — what the course covers and how it is organised, with every topic linked to its slide deck.

General information

CourseCSE 203 · Digital Logic Design · theory course
TextbookM. Morris Mano and Michael D. Ciletti, Digital Design, 5th edition, Pearson
MaterialsThe class lecture materials, rebuilt here as 13 interactive decks

What you will be able to do

  • Convert between number systems and use binary codes (BCD, Gray, ASCII) and signed arithmetic.
  • Simplify Boolean functions with the laws of Boolean algebra, canonical forms and Karnaugh maps.
  • Design combinational circuits: adders, subtractors, comparators, decoders, encoders, multiplexers and demultiplexers.
  • Analyse and design sequential circuits: latches, flip-flops, state machines, counters and registers.
  • Explain memory organisation, error detection and correction, ROM and programmable logic.
